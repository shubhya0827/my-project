About Jijamata Nursing School

Jijamata Nursing School is dedicated to preparing competent and compassionate nurses through a balanced blend of classroom teaching, simulation-based learning, and clinical training in reputed hospitals.

The curriculum emphasizes patient-centered care, ethical practice, and lifelong learning to meet the growing demands of modern healthcare systems.

  • • Experienced and student-friendly faculty members
  • • Outcome-based curriculum aligned with modern healthcare needs
  • • Safe, supportive, and inclusive campus environment
Vision

To develop nursing professionals who deliver safe, ethical, and evidence-based care.

Mission

To provide quality nursing education with a focus on clinical competence and human values.

Core Values

Compassion • Integrity • Professionalism • Teamwork.

Students are encouraged to adopt patient-first attitudes and uphold high standards of nursing practice at all times.

Courses Offered

General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM)

A comprehensive program that prepares students for a wide range of roles in hospitals, community health centers, and clinics.

  • Duration: 3 years
  • Eligibility: 10+2 (Science preferred)
  • Includes supervised clinical postings
Auxiliary Nurse Midwife (ANM)

Focuses on maternal and child health, community-based care, and primary healthcare delivery.

  • Duration: 2 years
  • Eligibility: 10+2 (any stream)
  • Ideal for community health services
Certificate & Value-added Programs

Short-term training in key areas to enhance skills and employability of nursing students.

  • Basic life support (BLS)
  • Infection control practices
  • Communication & soft skills

Campus & Facilities

Modern Laboratories

Well-equipped nursing foundation, anatomy, and community health labs for hands-on practice and demonstration.

Library & E-resources

Access to standard nursing textbooks, journals, and digital learning resources with quiet study spaces.

Clinical Tie-ups

Rotational postings in partnered hospitals to build clinical competence and confidence in patient care.

Hostel & Support

Safe hostel facilities, mentorship, and student support services for overall well-being and growth.

Admissions

Eligibility & Process
  • Applicants must have passed 10+2 from a recognized board.
  • Minimum aggregate percentage as per regulatory guidelines.
  • Selection may include entrance test and/or personal interview.
  • Medical fitness certificate required at the time of admission.

Detailed admission notifications, important dates, and fee structure will be published on the notice board and official channels.
